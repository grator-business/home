Our origin

Technology projects are where we started —
and still the thread that runs through everything we do.

Grator's roots run deep in telecommunications: our founder and chairman, Arturo Grau, is a telecommunications engineer, and that background has shaped how we think about technology from day one.

We develop technology projects that apply hands-on experience to create added value — differentiated ventures built to stand out in the market. That same conviction now extends across every area of Grator.
