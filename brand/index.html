The mark, the colours and the type, and how to use them.

Mark

The symbol is a geometric G, built only from straight lines of equal weight. The top end and the crossbar are cut at 45 degrees. In the colour version, the crossbar is the only note of colour.

The wordmark is GRATOR, set in Outfit, in capitals. Use the symbol on its own, or with the wordmark in a horizontal lockup.

Versions

Colour on a light ground, the version for dark grounds, and one colour only.

Clear space and size

Leave an empty margin around the mark equal to the thickness of the vertical stroke. Do not place text, rules or other marks inside it.

The dashed line is the edge of the clear space. The gap is the same on every side, and it matches the vertical stroke.

Smallest size: the symbol at 16 pixels, so it still reads as a G. The horizontal lockup should not be set below 24 pixels in height.

Use

  • Use the horizontal lockup, or the symbol alone. Do not invent a stacked version.
  • On a dark ground, use the dark-background files. The crossbar stays oxide.
  • When only one colour is possible, use the monochrome files. As an image they are black; inline, they follow the text colour.
  • Do not rotate, stretch, outline, shadow or recolour the mark.
  • Do not redraw the G with a typeface, and do not set GRATOR in another face.
  • Do not place the mark on a photograph or a busy background.
